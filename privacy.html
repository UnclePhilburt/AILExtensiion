Privacy & data use

IMPACT Companion is a team tool, not an official IMPACT or Globe Life product.

What the tool handles

The extension reads the selected IMPACT lead’s name, contact details, request type and activity history, plus any next lead it preloads. Phone actions can navigate IMPACT, register a call and submit supported call results.

Where information goes

In Cloud mode, this information and pending commands are sent over HTTPS to the administrator’s Supabase project. Access requires a signed-in, approved account. Each account can access only its own workspace. GitHub Pages hosts the app files; customer details are not committed to GitHub.

Local mode uses your computer bridge. Supabase still verifies your account, but local-mode lead data stays on your computer and phone.

Storage and removal

Cloud mode keeps the latest lead snapshot, not a growing lead archive (calendar entries, below, are the exception). The database setup includes a cleanup job for snapshots older than 30 minutes, running once per minute. Database backups follow the administrator’s Supabase retention settings. Account sessions remain in browser storage until sign-out. Diagnostic snapshots and lead-list caches may remain in extension storage until sign-out or account change.

Calendar

In Cloud mode the phone’s Calendar keeps the appointments and callbacks you schedule: the lead’s name, one phone number, address, request type, the scheduled time and the IMPACT status line it came from. They are stored under your account only (no one else can read them) and deleted automatically 30 days after the scheduled date. Local mode does not save calendar entries to the cloud.

Your choices

Use Options to disable automatic syncing or choose Local mode. Sign out when finished on a shared device. Contact the person who invited you for access removal, account deletion, or privacy questions. Never share account passwords or connection tokens.
