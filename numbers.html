Estimated from your calls

After 8 logged results, each number gets an estimate: Steady, Mixed, or Wearing out. Answered means a refusal or an appointment was logged. No answer means nobody picked up. This is not a carrier spam label. The spam-list forms are still the way to ask Verizon, T-Mobile, AT&T, and Sprint to clear a number.

Counts start when tracking is enabled and include calls registered through Companion. An appointment counts after its time is selected. These are recorded actions, not verified call connections or completed appointments. Earlier calls cannot be assigned retroactively.
