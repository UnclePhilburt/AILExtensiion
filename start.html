Download extension ↓
  1. Open brave://extensions in Brave or chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select the extracted extension folder.

A one-time browser step. No store, Node.js, bridge window, or connection token needed.

Updating & troubleshooting

To update, close IMPACT tabs, replace the files inside your existing extension folder with the new download, then click Reload on your browser’s extensions page. Reopen IMPACT.

Use one active computer per account. To switch computers, close IMPACT on the old one and wait 45 seconds. If your account needs access, contact your administrator.

Extensions installed this way do not update automatically. Your administrator will send this link when an update is ready.
