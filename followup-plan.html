How your plan works

Install Companion 0.4.31 on your computer, reload your IMPACT inbox, then click the green “Import inbox” button at the top of that page. On Sundays it imports automatically. Other days, use “Import inbox into follow-up plan.” Keep the inbox open until it finishes. Then open any lead in each IMPACT window so the phone controls can connect.

Sunday: intro text. Monday–Friday: up to three calls, at least two hours apart. Tuesday and Thursday also have a follow-up text. Saturday: final text and up to two calls.

Central time: weekdays 2–9 PM, Saturday 9 AM–2 PM, Sunday texts 6–9 PM. Meetings block your queue; callbacks do not. Missed calls expire each day.

Each texting day has its own A/B/C/D test. You still send each text and place each call. Only confirmed results are saved. Texts confirmed as sent also queue the IMPACT call-counter update, as in texting mode. Set your name on the Settings page before starting.

Missed Sunday? With the Monday fallback SQL update installed, unsent introductions become available Monday 10 AM–1 PM Central. Calls keep their normal 2–9 PM window, with attempts at 2, 5, and 8 PM. Leads already texted Sunday do not get another introduction.

Weekly reset update: run this SQL update once. It clears Cody’s current list and enables Sunday noon Central cleanup of previous weeks. Appointments, sent texts, and replied/stopped protections are kept. New Sunday imports remain in the list.

Database setup: run the setup SQL in your Supabase SQL editor once.
