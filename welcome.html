A private calling workspace. Not affiliated with IMPACT, AIL, or Globe Life. An administrator has to invite you before you can sign in.

CURRENT LEAD

The lead open on your computer appears on your phone. Record no answer, a refusal, or a virtual appointment, then move to the next person.

CALENDAR

Keep the week.

Appointments and callbacks stay on a month view. Afterward you can mark one held, a no-show, or rescheduled, and record APL and referrals.

YOUR DAY

See today.

A quiet summary of calls, results, and how appointments turned out, with statistics by day, week, month, and year.

ALONGSIDE

Sit with your team.

Totals only, side by side, from today through all time. Customer names, phone numbers, and notes stay off that page.

How you get in

Install the extension from the private Chrome Web Store link your administrator sends. Open the invitation, set a password and your first name, then sign in on the computer and on the phone. The same account works on both, even on different networks.
